3. Regulatory reality (why the platform is positioned as general wellness)

ItemState
FDA General Wellness policyLow-risk wellness devices/apps with non-disease claims = no premarket review (compliance path)
FTC Health Products Guidance (Dec 2024)Health/detox claims need competent & reliable scientific evidence; "detox" attracts scrutiny
FTC enforcement precedentTeami LLC (2020): $15.2M judgment over unsubstantiated detox claims
23andMe (Mar 2025)Chapter 11 — cautionary signal for DTC testing models
Positioning consequenceBENNETTAI211 publishes NO removal/detox outcome claims, NO diagnostic claims, NO device. Awareness + education + research-grounded guidance only.
This positioning is deliberate and enforced: "detox" is the most enforcement-prone claim in consumer health. The product that survives is the one that never makes it.
What this does NOT do Not a medical device. Not a microplastic test or biomonitoring service. Not a detox, removal, or treatment product. No risk scores, no individualized exposure estimates, no health data collection, no health-outcome claims of any kind. The science is contested (WHO 2022; 2026 critiques of headline studies) and the platform says so in every relevant briefing.
