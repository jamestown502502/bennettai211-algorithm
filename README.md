## What this is

BENNETTAI211 is a general-wellness awareness platform for microplastic exposure: five AI agents (Exposure Tracker, Detox Coach, Health Correlation, Research Integration, Community Engagement) deliver sourced, dated, honestly-caveated briefings based on lifestyle presets. It is NOT a device, NOT a test, NOT a detox product, and makes NO health claims.

## Positioning (non-negotiable)

"Detox" is the most enforcement-prone claim in consumer health. BENNETTAI211 publishes NO removal/detox outcome claims, NO diagnostic claims, NO device, NO test. Awareness + education + research-grounded guidance only. The contested science (WHO 2022; 2026 critiques) is disclosed on every briefing, not hidden.

## Status

Browser-based awareness content; zero data collected. No consumer measurement product exists today (verified gap) — that is the validated opportunity and the reason claims discipline matters. See `methodology.md` §4.
